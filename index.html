World Energy Flow

A Systems View of Global Energy

A bilingual energy-analysis platform connecting market movements to infrastructure, policy, geopolitics, expectations, and the feedback mechanisms that shape global energy systems.

Visit live platform →View GitHub showcase
Purpose

Go beyond the price move.

World Energy Flow is designed to explain not only what happened in oil and gas markets, but how infrastructure constraints, policy decisions, geopolitical events, expectations, and feedback loops interact to create those outcomes.

Research continuity

Home of World of Energy.

The platform preserves the weekly World of Energy analysis series initiated through Sharif University of Technology's Energy Department in 2023 while expanding it into a broader systems-oriented research platform.

What I built

Publishing + analysis infrastructure

Bilingual publishing

English/Farsi publication architecture with RTL support and permanent issue pages.

Market coverage

Recurring analysis across major oil and natural-gas benchmarks including Brent, WTI, Henry Hub, TTF, and JKM.

Historical dashboard

Price history, returns, volatility, rolling averages, and event-context views.

Downloadable data

Public market datasets accompany analysis so readers can inspect and reuse the underlying observations.

Systems interpretation

Market commentary is structured around interacting drivers rather than isolated headlines.

Research platform

The architecture is designed to expand into energy security, transition policy, System Dynamics, scenarios, and interactive models.

Research standard

Observation, calculation, inference, scenario.

The publication distinguishes between sourced observations, calculated metrics, analytical inference, forecasts or scenarios, and limitations. This keeps the evidence layer separate from interpretation.
