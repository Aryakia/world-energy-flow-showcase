Purpose

Explain the system behind the price move.

World Energy Flow is designed to move from market observation to causal interpretation. Price is treated as an outcome of interacting supply, demand, inventories, infrastructure constraints, expectations, policy, and geopolitical events.

Research continuity

Home of World of Energy.

The platform preserves the weekly World of Energy series initiated through Sharif University of Technology's Energy Department while expanding it into a broader systems-oriented research environment.

Editorial method

Keep evidence and interpretation separate.

Sourced observation
What a market or reliable source reports.
Calculated metric
Transparent transformation of available observations.
Analytical inference
Interpretation of interacting drivers.
Forecast / scenario
Explicit forward-looking conditional analysis.
Limitation
Missing evidence, uncertainty, or scope boundary.
Systems lens

Markets are feedback systems.

Analysis can connect production, storage, transport capacity, demand adaptation, substitution, sanctions, policy, geopolitical disruptions, expectations, and implementation delays rather than reducing explanation to a single headline.

Direction

From weekly market analysis to a broader energy lab.

The architecture can expand into energy security, transition policy, country analysis, System Dynamics, policy scenarios, and interactive models when substantive research content is ready.

Disclosure boundary

Public analysis, private production source.

The showcase excludes credentials, deployment configuration, internal editorial workflow, unpublished analysis, and non-public data. Public datasets are intentionally public; private operational material stays outside this repository.
