## Project overview

World Energy Flow is a bilingual energy-analysis platform that connects market movements to the physical infrastructure, policy choices, geopolitical events, expectations, and feedback mechanisms that produce them.

It is also the permanent digital home of **World of Energy**, a weekly energy-analysis series initiated through Sharif University of Technology's Energy Department in 2023.

## What I built

- English/Farsi publication interface with RTL support
- Recurring oil and natural-gas market briefs
- Coverage of benchmarks including Brent, WTI, Henry Hub, Dutch TTF, and JKM
- Permanent digital archive of World of Energy issues
- Historical price dashboard and analytical views
- Downloadable market datasets
- RSS, sitemap, publication calendar, and structured metadata
- Systems-oriented interpretation of market drivers

## Analytical approach

The platform distinguishes between sourced observations, calculated metrics, analytical inference, forecasts/scenarios, and limitations. The objective is not only to report what energy prices did, but to explain the interacting mechanisms that may have produced those movements.

## Long-term direction

World Energy Flow is designed to grow into a broader research environment covering energy security, transition policy, System Dynamics, scenarios, country analysis, and interactive models.
